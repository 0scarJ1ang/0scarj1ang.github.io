About

I am a PhD student at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Harold Soh.

Before joining NUS, I completed a B.A. in Engineering and an M.Eng. at the University of Cambridge, followed by an M.Res. at Imperial College London, where I was supervised by Dr. Nicole Salomons. I received First Class Honours / Distinction across my previous degrees.

Research

My research focuses on robot learning, with an emphasis on building good robot policies: policies that work beyond their demonstrations, and that high-level reasoning can understand and invoke correctly.

Policies That Generalize and Adapt

A policy is trained once, but its objectives keep changing after deployment. How can it generalize beyond its demonstrations and adapt to new objectives without retraining?

Policies That Planners Can Understand and Compose

Planners usually see a skill only through a name or an instruction. How can high-level reasoning understand what a policy can do and where it works, and invoke it correctly?
